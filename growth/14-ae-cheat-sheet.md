# 14 · The sales rep's cheat sheet

The first person at a startup to hear "do you support SCIM?" is usually an
account executive on a call, not an engineer. They either bluff or say "I'll
check" and lose momentum. Give them a one-page answer sheet: what to say today,
what never to say, and a link to send engineering. Sales communities share
tools like this widely, and every rep who uses it becomes the person who
forwards ToUpper to their CTO.

**Effort:** done, plus 2 posts · **First signal:** week 2 · **Measure:** leads with `campaign = h14_cheatsheet`

The cheat sheet is [`kits/ae-cheat-sheet.md`](kits/ae-cheat-sheet.md). Every
estimate in it comes from `data/glossary.js`. Export it as a PDF (open it in
any Markdown editor and print to PDF) for sharing.

## Step by step

1. **Export the PDF** and put it on a public link (Google Drive "anyone with
   the link", or the hack 11 repo).
2. **Post in sales communities** that allow resources. Many have a
   "tools/resources" channel; use that and not the general channel.
3. **Post on LinkedIn** with the copy below, the PDF uploaded as a document post
   (these get swiped through, page by page).
4. **Offer a 15-minute "what did they ask for?" call** to any rep who replies
   with a live deal. Those turn into the best leads you'll get, because
   there's a buyer waiting.
