# 06 · Tell the vendors they're listed

Every topic with a buy option links three platforms. That's 55 companies whose
buyers are reading a neutral page that recommends them at the exact moment of
purchase. Vendors rarely get that from a third party, and most will happily
share it.

**Effort:** 2 hours · **First signal:** week 2 · **Measure:** leads with `campaign = h06_listed`

## Step by step

1. **Start with the vendors listed under the most topics** (top of the table below).
2. **Find the right inbox:** partnerships, content marketing, or developer
   relations. Most have a partners page or a marketing lead on LinkedIn.
3. **Send the email below** with the exact link for their topic.
4. **If they share it, thank them publicly** and repost. If they ask to be
   listed under more topics, only agree where it fits.
5. **Never accept payment for placement** unless the page labels it as sponsored.
   The list stays editorial or it stops being worth sharing.

## The email

> **Subject:** [Vendor] is one of three platforms we recommend for [topic]
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Quick heads-up rather than a pitch. ToUpper has a short guide to what
> enterprise buyers ask for, and for [topic] it names three platforms worth a
> look. [Vendor] is one of them:
>
> [link to /what/topic?mode=buy with the h06_listed tag]
>
> People land on it when a customer has just asked for [topic], so it's
> reaching buyers at a useful moment. Nobody paid to be on it.
>
> If it's useful for your newsletter, docs, or a post, feel free to share. And
> if anything about [Vendor] is described wrong, tell me and I'll fix it.
>
> [Your name]

### Follow-up, one week later

> **Subject:** Re: [Vendor] is one of three platforms we recommend for [topic]
>
> One thing that might be handy for your team: [topic] also comes as a Slack
> command now, `/toupper [topic]`, so it turns up when prospects are
> discussing it internally. Happy to send the install link.
>
> [Your name]
