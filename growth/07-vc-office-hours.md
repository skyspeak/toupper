# 07 · VC platform office hours

VC platform teams field "our portfolio company needs SOC 2 / SSO / an AI
policy" every week and usually answer with an introduction. Give them
something better to send, plus a 30-minute session for the portfolio. One yes
reaches dozens of companies at the moment they're blocked.

**Effort:** 1 email, 1 session · **First signal:** week 3 · **Measure:** leads with `campaign = h07_officehours`

## Step by step

1. **Pick 20 seed and Series A funds** with a visible platform or portfolio
   success team. Their websites usually list them under "Platform", "Portfolio"
   or "Team".
2. **Email the platform lead** (below). One email, one follow-up.
3. **When one says yes,** book 30 minutes and send them the portfolio post.
4. **Run the session** using the run-of-show.
5. **Send the follow-up** within 24 hours, then ask for one introduction to
   another fund's platform team.

## Run of show (30 minutes)

| Minutes | What |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Why deals stall after signature: the ten weeks, told as one story |
| 3–8 | The three decisions that save the most time: scope, build or buy, and what the customer will accept in the meantime |
| 8–25 | Hot seat: two or three founders share a blocker, you open the topic page live, adjust the factors to their case, and walk the questions |
| 25–30 | Where to go next: the guide, the Slack command, and how to reach you |
