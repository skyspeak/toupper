The shift

The people who built this stuff are no longer inside big companies.

A generation of product and platform leaders spent five to fifteen years taking SaaS companies upmarket. They shipped the identity layer, rebuilt the billing engine, survived the FedRAMP conversation. Many have since gone independent — a practice of one, a small studio, two days a week.

Their expertise is unusually narrow and unusually transferable. The problems repeat almost exactly from company to company, which is why an outside operator who has done it three times can be worth more than a year of internal discovery.
