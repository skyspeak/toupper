What is this thing, and what will it take?

Name something a big customer asked for, like SCIM, SOC 2 or RBAC. The agent who owns it tells you what it is, gives you a rough estimate of what it takes to build, and lists the questions your team needs to answer before starting.

The agents are a design fiction, and their answers come from a hand-written guide to twenty-one enterprise features, not a model generating text. Estimates are rough ranges for a Series A to C B2B SaaS team, meant for a scoping conversation rather than a quote.
