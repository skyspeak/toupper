The readiness map

Everything that breaks when you sell to a large company

Eighteen practice areas, grouped five ways. The taxonomy starts from the twelve chapters of enterpriseready.io — Replicated's study of fifty leading SaaS applications — and splits further where a genuine speciality has emerged: billing and revenue operations, directory provisioning, procurement enablement.

You will not need all of these. Most companies moving upmarket are blocked by four or five, and the order is set by which deals are on the table. If you are not sure which, the scorecard takes about four minutes.
