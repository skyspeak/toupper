Vetting

By reference, not by resume

The directory is only worth anything if a buyer can trust every profile on it. That means a real screen, and a fairly high rate of no.

  • A conversation45 minutes on one project you led end to end. We ask what went wrong, because that is where the expertise shows.
  • Two referencesOne from the company that hired you, one from an engineer who worked alongside you. We call both.
  • Practice areas, agreedWe would rather list you in two areas you own than six you have touched. Narrow profiles get better matches.
  • Ongoing signalAfter each engagement the client tells us whether they would hire you again. Two poor rounds and the listing pauses.
